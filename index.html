    How pricing works

    Every catalogue price is the publisher's list price in the local currency of a market. Change Market and the same tier is re-quoted — Spotify Individual is ₹119 in India and $11.99 in the United States, and that is a real difference in what the publisher charges, not a currency conversion.

    Where a publisher's exact local price isn't in the catalogue, the price is estimated from the US price and that market's typical price level for digital subscriptions, then converted. Estimates are marked est.

    Show prices in only changes the currency the numbers are displayed in. It does not change which market's price is being quoted.

    Catalogue prices are a best-effort snapshot and move constantly. Every tracked subscription keeps its own editable amount — set it to what you actually pay.
